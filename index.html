Questions

Answers Before You Ask

The things property managers and owners usually want to know first.

Have a question that isn’t covered here? Send it over or call 361-876-7822 — calls and quote requests are welcome 24/7.

What information do you need to provide a quote?

The property address, the services you are interested in, and a general idea of the size or condition of the lot are a good start. Photos help, and a short site visit lets us confirm the scope before you receive a final number.

Do you work around business operating hours?

Yes. Much of our striping and maintenance work is scheduled during off-peak hours, overnight, or in phases so your customers and tenants keep access to the property while we work.

Can you restripe an existing parking lot?

Yes. Restriping over an existing layout is one of our most common projects. We can also adjust the layout first if your current one wastes space or creates traffic problems.

Do you install wheel stops and parking signs?

Yes. We install concrete and rubber wheel stops, plus reserved parking, accessible parking, tow-away zone, and general traffic signs.

Do you provide accessible parking markings?

Yes. We paint accessible stalls, access aisles, and related markings according to the property scope and applicable requirements. A site review lets us confirm what your specific lot needs.

How long does parking lot paint take to dry?

Traffic paint is typically dry to the touch quickly and ready for vehicles shortly after application, depending on temperature and humidity. We coordinate the schedule so freshly painted areas are protected until they are ready.

Which areas do you serve?

Coastal Roadworks serves Houston, Harris County, Montgomery County, and surrounding areas.

Do you handle both repairs and striping?

Yes. Handling asphalt repairs, crack filling, and striping together usually saves time and money, and the finished lot looks and performs better when the work is coordinated.
