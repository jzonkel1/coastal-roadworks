The Work

Patched Flat, Compacted to Last.

A pothole is never just a pothole. Water gets under the surface, the edges crumble, and a repair that would have taken an afternoon turns into a resurfacing line item. We cut out the failed area, rebuild it with hot or cold-mix asphalt as the job calls for, and compact it so the patch performs like the pavement around it.

Repairs are coordinated with striping and marking work whenever possible — one mobilization, one schedule, and a lot that comes back online looking finished instead of patched.

What’s Included

What asphalt repair covers

Pothole repair

Failed areas cut back to sound pavement, filled, and compacted — not topped off with a shovel of cold patch that washes out in a month.

Section patching

Larger failed sections removed and rebuilt, matched to the height of the surrounding pavement for clean drainage and a smooth ride.

Trip-hazard removal

Heaved, crumbled, or washed-out areas around walkways, driveways, drive lanes, and dumpster pads brought back to a safe, even surface.

Sealcoating

A fresh protective coat over sound pavement — it shields the surface from UV, water, and fuel, evens out the color so new striping reads sharp against it, and buys years before a lot needs resurfacing.

Repair + restripe packages

Patches restriped to match the existing layout, so the repair disappears into the lot instead of standing out.

Asphalt & Pothole Repair FAQs

Straight answers to what property managers and owners ask us most.

Anything else? Send it over or call 361-876-7822.

How fast can a pothole be repaired?

Most individual potholes are cut, filled, and compacted in a single short visit, and the area can typically take traffic again the same day.

Will the patch match the rest of the lot?

The patch is compacted level with the surrounding pavement, and any markings that crossed the repair are re-painted so the finished area blends in.

Do you sealcoat as well as repair?

Yes. Sealcoating goes over sound pavement, so repairs and crack filling come first — sealing over a failing surface just hides the problem for a season. On a full job we will cut out the bad areas, fill the cracks, seal, then stripe, and the lot comes back looking new rather than patched.

Do you repair concrete too?

Our core repair work is asphalt. If your project involves concrete, send the details through the quote form and we will tell you straight whether it is work we take on.

How do I know if I need patching or full resurfacing?

If damage is isolated — potholes, a failed section near a drain, alligatored spots — patching usually makes sense. If most of the surface is failing, we will say so instead of selling you patches.
